Overview

Mandukaparni (Centella asiatica) is a perennial, creeping herb widely found in moist and shady places. It is a well-known Medhya (brain tonic) and Rasayana drug in Ayurveda. Traditionally used for enhancing memory, intellect and longevity, it is also valued for its wound healing, anti-inflammatory and neuroprotective properties.

🌿  Key Highlights

  • Classical Ayurvedic Medhya Rasayana
  • Supports memory and cognitive function
  • Useful in skin disorders and wound healing
  • Adaptogenic and anti-stress properties
  • Widely studied for neuroprotective and antioxidant effects
