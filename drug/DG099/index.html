Botanical Description & Identification
Habit
Large tree; commonly described with a broad crown and often buttressed trunk.
Bark
Smooth, pale greenish to whitish-grey bark.
Leaves
Simple, usually subopposite, oblong-elliptic to somewhat sub-orbicular leaves; petiole with glands near its apex.
Flowers
Small, yellowish-white flowers in axillary or terminal inflorescences.
Fruit
Brown, ovoid-oblong fruit with five prominent projecting wings.

Hridroga, Vrana, Agnimandya, Jwara, Shotha, Kushtha and Arsha among reported classical indications.

Arjunolic acid, arjunic acid, arjungenin, gallic acid, ellagic acid and quercetin are among compounds reported in published reviews.
