Botanical Description & Identification
Habit
Large tree; commonly described as a tree with a broad crown and often buttressed trunk.
Bark
Smooth, pale greenish to whitish-grey bark.
Leaves
Simple, usually subopposite, oblong-elliptic to somewhat sub-orbicular leaves; petiole with rounded glands near its apex.
Flowers
Small, yellowish-white flowers in axillary or terminal inflorescences.
Fruit
Brown, ovoid-oblong fruit with five prominent projecting wings.
