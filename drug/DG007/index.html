Morphology

Sharma describes Bhanga as an annual herb reaching about 8 feet, with thin, tender branches. The leaves are alternate and palmately divided, with about 3–7 leaflets on a petiole. Flowers are small and occur in clusters; the fruit is small, round and granular.

The source distinguishes the tender branches bearing leaves and seeds as Bhanga, the resinous inflorescence of the female plant as Ganja, and the resinous material deposited on the branches as Charas.

Distribution & Chemical Composition

The book records occurrence in India, Iran, Iraq and Egypt, with Bihar, Bengal and Uttar Pradesh particularly mentioned in India. Sharma lists resin, cannabinone, volatile oil, gum/resinous matter, fat, sugar, wax and potassium nitrate among the constituents.

System-wise Karma

External: Sharma describes warming, pain-relieving effects of local application and fomentation; leaf juice is also described as having external antiparasitic action.

Nervous system: The source describes Bhanga as an intoxicating drug. It records an initial pleasurable state followed by delirium, and states that appropriately used it has analgesic, sleep-inducing and anticonvulsant actions.

Digestive system: It is described as Deepana, Pachana, Rochana, Grahi and Pittasara, with antispasmodic and colic-relieving effects.

Blood and circulation: The source describes an early stimulatory phase followed by slowing of the pulse and notes an astringent haemostatic action.

Respiratory system: It is described as reducing bronchial spasm and as useful in cough and dyspnoea in the source.

Urinary system: Sharma notes an increase in urine output.

Reproductive system: The source describes seminal stambhana and increased uterine contraction.

Skin/sensation: It describes reduced sensory nerve function with tingling and numbness.

Therapeutic Applications in the Source
  • Selected Vata-Kapha disorders
  • Headache and half-sided headache-type pain
  • Insomnia and selected convulsive disorders
  • Selected abdominal pain, indigestion and diarrhoeal conditions
  • Selected bleeding conditions
  • Selected cough and dyspnoea conditions
  • Selected urinary disorders and urinary retention
  • Selected conditions involving seminal stambhana and uterine contraction
